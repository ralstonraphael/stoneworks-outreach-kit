# Before and after

## Sharing thoughts on a teammate's brief

Before (memo voice):
> Hi team, I wanted to extend my gratitude for the comprehensive client brief. I have reviewed it in detail and consolidated my observations below. Additionally, I would like to highlight several areas where I believe adjustments could further optimize our approach.

After:
> hey, went through the client brief and merged it with my notes into a 2 pager (attached). thanks for putting it together, really useful
>
> my thoughts:
> - love the people list and vendor table. confirms we skip title and runsheets
> - how they bill decides the pitch. day rate means we lead with invoice checks, per tract means status reports
>
> can someone double check the names before thursday?

## Asking for help

Before: "Hope you're well! I was wondering if you might possibly have a moment to take a look at the deck when you get a chance, no rush at all."
After: "can you look at the deck before friday? mostly want a gut check on slide 4"

## Disagreeing

Before: "I respectfully disagree with the proposed 4 to 6 week timeline, as it may not be optimal."
After: "I'd lean 2 to 4 weeks instead of 4 to 6. smaller scope, faster result we can point to. open to pushback"

## Status update

Before: "Quick update: Progress has been made on several fronts. In conclusion, we are on track."
After: "all 8 follow-ups went out, 1 reply so far (Dana, wants a call next week). nothing else needed from you yet"

## Not this skill

A message to a client or investor needs `deslop` or `cold-email`: fuller sentences, no lowercase, a real signature.
