Ask on call 1

  1. How do you bill: day rate, per tract, fixed fee?
  2. Which AI tools do you use for people and projects, and what is missing?
  3. Walk me through your last project, win to payment.
  4. Where do hours go that you cannot bill?
  5. What usually threatens a spud date?

Good sign: weekly, 5+ hours, same shape each time, documents exist, a human reviews, a named owner and metric.
